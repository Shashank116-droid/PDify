Delete Your PDify Account

If you wish to delete your account and all associated data from PDify, you can do so directly within the mobile app:

  1. Open PDify.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

Can't access the app?

If you cannot access the app, you may request account deletion by emailing us with your registered email address.

Email Subject: PDify Account Deletion Request

Body: Please delete my account associated with [Your Email].


Email Support

Data deletion is permanent and includes all your uploaded files and profile information.
