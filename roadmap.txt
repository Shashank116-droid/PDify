PDify Future Roadmap v1.2.0 & Beyond
-------------------------------------

1. Voice Interaction
   - Voice-to-Chat (STT) for hands-free questioning.
   - Summaries-to-Voice (TTS) to listen while multitasking.

2. Multi-Document Intelligence
   - Ask questions across collections of PDFs (Knowledge Bases).
   - Comparative analysis between documents.

3. Multimodal & OCR
   - Support for scanned images and handwriting using Gemini Vision.
   - Scan physical docs directly into the app.

4. Productivity & Organization
   - Group PDFs into Folders or Project categories.
   - Deep search (search within summary content).
   - Export chat histories/summaries to PDF/Word.

5. Insights
   - "Time Saved" dashboard visualizing AI productivity.
   - Topic clouds for common themes in your documents.
